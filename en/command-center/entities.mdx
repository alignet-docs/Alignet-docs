In **Gestión de entidades**, use the **Adquirentes**, **Payment Facilitators** and **Gateways** tabs. Viewing and administrative access depend on your role and organization. Control names match the original interface.

## Acquirers

The list offers name search, status, filters, columns and details. **Nuevo adquirente** opens seven stages:

1. General details.
2. Tax information.
3. Contacts and banks.
4. Services.
5. Processing.
6. Capture.
7. Review.

**Datos generales** shows name, country, acquirer ID, international response capture and status. The screen indicates that the code, alias and keys are generated during registration.

## Payment facilitators

Search by name and use status and filters. **Nuevo PF** has eight stages: parent acquirer, general details, tax information, contacts and banks, services, processing, capture and review.

Select the authorized parent acquirer first. Available processors, brands and methods depend on its enabled capabilities.

## Gateways

The list offers **Filtros**, **Buscar** and **Nuevo gateway**. The wizard includes general details, tax information, contacts, credentials and review.

The first step asks for name and status. The screen indicates that a new gateway is created active by default.

<Warning>Do not share keys or credentials. Configuration and final registration must be performed by authorized staff.</Warning>
