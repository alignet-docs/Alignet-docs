The Alignet Integrations team registers the payment-form appearance. The merchant must provide:

* A PNG logo sized **300 × 90 px**.
* Background colors in hexadecimal format.
* Option-selector color.
* Primary and selected button colors.
* Text-link color.

<Frame caption="Official Redirect payment-form customization reference">
  <img src="/images/vpos2-redirect-personalizacion.png" alt="Redirect form with customizable elements" />
</Frame>

<Note>
  Designs combine two or three colors, primarily visible in the form header. Provide exact hexadecimal values and review text contrast before approving the design.
</Note>
