Dynamic reports let users select the fields or tags they need and download order information.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the download option">From the orders screen, open the **Download** query.</Step>
  <Step title="Design the report">Select the required fields and avoid fields that the merchant does not send during processing.</Step>
  <Step title="Generate the file">Download the result as `.txt`, `.xls`, or `.csv`.</Step>
</Steps>

<Frame caption="Configuring and generating a dynamic report">
  ![Generating dynamic reports in VPayment Web](/images/vpayment-web/reportes-dinamicos.gif)
</Frame>

## Limits and recommendations

- A report can include up to **100,000 records**.
- Excel data is split into sheets of **12,000 records**.
- You do not need to wait for a confirmation email.
- Select only necessary fields; fields not sent by the merchant appear empty.
