VPayment Web is the administrative system used by authorized merchant users to review and operate on transactions recorded during authorization.

<Warning>
  This guide does not apply to merchants in Peru. V-Payment is offered regionally and procedures may vary by country. To confirm the appropriate process, contact [integraciones@alignet.com](mailto:integraciones@alignet.com).
</Warning>

## What you can do

- Review order details and current status.
- Identify decline reasons or invalid data.
- Reverse authorized orders, subject to permissions and acquirer rules.
- Prepare batches for Base II processing or settlement.
- Generate customized reports.
- Manage users and access.
