For guidance with VPOS integration, configuration, or testing, contact the Alignet Integrations team.

<Card title="Alignet Integrations" icon="envelope" href="mailto:integraciones@alignet.com">
  integraciones@alignet.com
</Card>

## Recommended information

Include the following details to help the team review your request:

- Country and merchant or organization name.
- Solution in use: Ecommerce, Modal, Redirect, SDK, or VPayment Web.
- Environment where the issue occurs.
- Brief description of the observed behavior.
- Approximate operation date and time.
- Order identifier or available technical reference.
- Screenshots or logs that help reproduce the case.

<Warning>
  Do not email passwords, private keys, complete card numbers, security codes, or other sensitive information.
</Warning>

## Before contacting support

1. Review the solution requirements and considerations.
2. Confirm that you are using the correct environment credentials and URLs.
3. Keep the received message or result available as a reference.
