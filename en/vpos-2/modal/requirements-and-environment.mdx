## Business requirements

* An active or in-development website, or active social networks.
* A valid SSL certificate on the website.
* At least one technical person responsible for the integration.
* Confirmation from the acquirer that the Modal integration applies to the merchant and country.

## Published technical components

| Platform | Components |
| --- | --- |
| Java | Axis2, Jersey, and JSR/Jersey libraries included in the integration kit |
| C# / VB | `System.Text` and `System.Security.Cryptography` |
| PHP | PHP 5, OpenSSL 0.9.7 or later, HASH, XML, and SOAP |

<Note>
  These versions match the matrix published in the official guide. If you use a modern platform, confirm the current compatibility matrix with the Integrations team.
</Note>

## Integration environment

* Available 24 hours a day, Monday through Sunday.
* Support from 09:00 to 18:00 on business days.
* Times are expressed in Peru time, GMT-5.
* Test results must be sent to the responsible integration specialists.

## Published minimum compatibility

The guide covers Chrome and Firefox on PC, Chrome on Android, and Safari 10 or later on iPhone, iPad, and macOS. Also test the current versions used by your customers.

- [ ] HTTPS works without warnings.
- [ ] The merchant has a technical owner.
- [ ] The acquirer confirmed the integration method.
- [ ] Desktop and mobile devices will be tested.
