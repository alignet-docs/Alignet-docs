## Three important distinctions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Authorized does not mean settled or paid out">
    **Authorized:** the payment was approved. **Settled:** the operation is submitted to the card network for BASE2 processing. Settlement alone does not confirm a deposit into the merchant's account.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Cancelled is not the same as reversed">
    **Cancelled:** the user cancels before authorization. **Reversed:** an authorized operation is voided during the first day and before settlement. The amount returned takes applicable administrative fees into account.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Declined, invalid and expired mean different things">
    **Declined:** the issuer rejected the payment. **Invalid:** a rule, validation or error prevented it from continuing. **Expired:** the deadline passed without a customer response, depending on the payment method. None of these attempts was authorized, but the reasons differ.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
