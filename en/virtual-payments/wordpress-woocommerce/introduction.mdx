Alignet One WooCommerce an official plugin for **Recovery at your store WordPress** with the most important payment methods from local market (Peru and region) from a single integration.

### Unavailable payment methods

Depending on your configuration, you can enable:

* **Cards:** Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Dines.
* **Cash bills:** YapePlin.
* **Payments with QR.**
* **Transfers and Cash:** PagoEfectivo, Cuotéalo BCP.

### General Flow of Payment

Understanding how information travels is key to a successful integration. The plugin manages the whole life cycle of the transaction automatically.

### How does that work?

1. **Selection:** The client chooses Alignet One as a payment method at the WooCommerce.
2. **Sending:** The plugin encoded the transaction information to the gateway Alignet One.
3. **Validation:** Alignet One Valids payment with issuers and processors under safety standards PCI-DSS.
4. **Answer:** - **Symchrone:** The client receives an immediate confirmation on screen.
   * **Asynchronous (Webhook):** Alignet One notify your store using a web hold to update the order status automatically.

***

## Requesting Life Cycle

To make your logistics efficient, the plugin mapes the states of the gateway with the native states of WooCommerce:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Registration and pending">
    The order will be created at WooCommerce how **"outstanding"**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Authorisation">
    If payment is successful, the state changes to **"Processing"** (or "Complete" as per your configuration), notifying the client by mail.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Failed or Abandoned">
    If the payment is rejected or the client closes the window, the order will be marked as **Failed** or **"Cancelled"**.
  </Step>
</Steps>
