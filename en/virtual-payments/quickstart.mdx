## From configuration to first payment

<Steps>
  <Step title="Obtain and protect your credentials">
    You need a **Merchant Code**, **Client ID** and **Client Secret**. Confirm the credentials and assigned environment with Alignet. If you register the merchant in Command Center, save the values displayed when registration is complete.

    Use a secrets manager or secure server configuration. Never expose the Client Secret in the browser, app, repository or logs. For plugins, restrict access to the admin panel where these values are entered.

    <Warning>Do not mix test and production credentials.</Warning>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure your selected channel">
    Follow the [E-commerce](/en/virtual-payments/e-commerce/introduction), [Web Checkout](/en/virtual-payments/checkout-web/introduction) or [Mobile SDK](/en/virtual-payments/sdk-mobile/introduction) guide.

    Configure the merchant, environment, amount, currency and URLs or notifications required by your integration. Use HTTPS and the documented parameters for that channel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Track each purchase and attempt">
    Link your order to the **operation** and retain the identifiers for each **transaction or attempt**. A retry must not create another sale in your store.

    Confirm the result through your integration's backend mechanism. The checkout screen or a visual callback alone is not enough to mark an order as paid. Process repeated notifications without duplicating actions.

    Review [Smart operations](/en/virtual-payments/payments/smart-operations) and [Transaction states](/en/virtual-payments/payments/transaction-states).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Run your first test payment">
    Use only [test data](/en/virtual-payments/payments/test-data) in the assigned environment. Check the amount, currency and order outcome.

    Also test declines, pending payments, expiration and controlled errors where applicable to the enabled method. Validate notifications, return flows and recovery from connection failures.

    Keep evidence and identifiers for support without recording secrets or sensitive card data.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Prepare to go live">
    Complete the [Production checklist](/en/virtual-payments/certification-checklist) and coordinate the review with Alignet.

    Run controlled production tests only with the appropriate credentials and authorization. Monitor errors, timeouts, unusual declines and order consistency when you start operating.
  </Step>
</Steps>
