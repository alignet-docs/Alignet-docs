## The advantage of a direct connection

Unlike other aggregators in the market, **Alignet is a direct acquirer and an official 3-D Secure technology provider**.

Centralizing your operation with us removes technical intermediaries. The result is faster processing, a substantial reduction in false declines, and a higher approval rate while operating under global payment network standards.
