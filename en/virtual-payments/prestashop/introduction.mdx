Alignet One PrestaShop is the official module for **Recovery at your store PrestaShop** with the most important payment methods from local market (Peru and region) from a single integration.

### Unavailable payment methods

Depending on your configuration, you can enable

* **Cards:** Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Dines.
* **Cash bills:** YapePlin.
* **Payments with QR.**
* **Transfers and Cash:** PagoEfectivo, Cuotéalo BCP.

### General Flow of Payment

Understanding how information travels is key to a successful integration. The module manages the entire life cycle of the transaction automatically.

<img src="https://mintcdn.com/alignet/o_w6plaWwLfsBKjg/images/image-66.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=o_w6plaWwLfsBKjg&q=85&s=3dd1e78c664bb3710f45ce45e7efa9fa" alt="Image" width="1595" height="672" data-path="images/image-66.png" />

### How does that work?

1. **Selection:** The client chooses Alignet One as a payment method at the PrestaShop.
2. **Sending:** The module emits the transaction information to the gateway Alignet One.
3. **Validation:** Alignet One Valids payment with issuers and processors under safety standards PCI-DSS.
4. **Answer:**
   * **Symchrone:** The client receives an immediate confirmation on screen.
   * **Asynchronous (Webhook):** Alignet One notify your store using a web hold to update the order status automatically.

***

## Requesting Life Cycle

To have your operation consisstore, the module maps the states of the gateway with the native states of PrestaShop:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Registration and pending">
    The order will be created at PrestaShop how **"Waiting for payment"** or **"Outstanding"**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Approved payment">
    If payment is successful, the state changes to **"Payment accepted"**, enabling them to start preparing their order.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Judgement or Cancellation">
    If payment's rejected or the client's leave the flow, the order's marked as **"Recovery error"** or **"Cancelled"**.
  </Step>
</Steps>
