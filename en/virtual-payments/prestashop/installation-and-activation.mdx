Before you start, make sure you have completed the phase of [Access to the Platform](/en/virtual-payments/prestashop/introduction) and have the file **.zip** module Alignet One for PrestaShop.

Install **Alignet One Gateway** through the native PrestaShop module manager for integration with your theme's hooks.

## Module Download

Download the latest version published by the Alignet One team:

<Card title="Download module Alignet One for PrestaShop" icon="download" href="https://github.com/alignet-docs/Alignet-docs/raw/refs/heads/main/downloads/plugins/prestashop-payme-charges-latam-v1.zip">
  Download **prestashop-payme-charges-latam-v1.zip**, the Pay-me module for PrestaShop.
</Card>

***

## Installation process

Follow these steps inside your management panel:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Access back office">
    login on your PrestaShop with an administrator's credentials.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Module Manager">
    On the left side menu, go to **Modules** > **Module Manager** (or Module Manager).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Upload module">
    Click **Upload a module** at the top right of the screen.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Select File">
    Drag the **prestashop-payme-charges-latam-v1.zip** file or select it from your computer. The system automatically uploads and extracts it.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Finish and Go to Setup">
    Once “Module installed” appears, click **Configure** to enter your credentials.
  </Step>
</Steps>

***

## Installation Verification

To confirm that the module is activated and recognised by the system:

1. Go to **Modules** > **Module Manager**.
2. At the search bar, write **"Alignet One"**.
3. Make sure the switch's at **activated**. If you see a button that says "Enabling," click on it.

<Card>
  **Failed up?** Check that your server allows uploading at least 10MB and that folder **/ modules /** of your installation PrestaShop have write permits **(755 or 775)**.
</Card>
