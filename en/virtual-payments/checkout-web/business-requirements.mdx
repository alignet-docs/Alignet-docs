---
title: "Business requirements"
description: "Requirements for getting started with Alignet One."
---
Requirements to start with Alignet One:

* Social Reason in Peru (Ficha) RUC 20 or 10)
* To have a website and / or be developing it. If you have no website, at least social networks have been activated.
* The web should have certification SSL.
* To have a bank account in the name of Social Reason.
* To have at least one person with responsibility for integration (programmer / Developer).
