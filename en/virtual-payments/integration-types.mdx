## Choose based on your project

| If your business has… | Choose… | What to prepare |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A WooCommerce, PrestaShop or VTEX store | E-commerce | Administrator access and merchant credentials. |
| A custom-built website | Web Checkout | Frontend and backend development, URLs and notifications. |
| An Android or iOS app | Mobile SDK | Mobile and backend development, credentials and test devices. |

The effort depends on your system, enabled methods and merchant validation requirements. Configuring a plugin does not remove your security and operational responsibilities.
