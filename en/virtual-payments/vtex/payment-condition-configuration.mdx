---
title: "Configure payment conditions in VTEX"
---
After creating the Affiliation of **Alignet One** and Loading Credentials, Assess to **Payment Conditions** from the store.

### 1) Access to Payment Conditions

At VTEX, go to: **Store Settings > Payments > Settings**.\
Select the option **Payment Conditions**.

### 2) Select the supplier Alignet One

On the list of payment methods, place **Flex**.\
Click to open the configuration.

Open full screen image

### 3) Configure condition

In **Process with provider**select the association of **Flex** previously created (example: *Alignet One Test pet*).\
Make sure his **Status** and **Active**.

<Frame>
  <img src="https://mintcdn.com/alignet/rK9j2XTEKg2v8ZME/images/image-57.png?fit=max&auto=format&n=rK9j2XTEKg2v8ZME&q=85&s=70c4b51e6238d45e11573e919024079f" alt="Image" width="1351" height="571" data-path="images/image-57.png" />

### 4) Save changes

Click **Save**.\
The changes can take about **5 to 10 minutes** To be applied to storage check-out.
