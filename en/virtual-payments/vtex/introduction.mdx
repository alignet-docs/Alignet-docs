## General description

with our payment Connector for VTEX you can integrate Alignet One quickly, safely and scalably, centralizing multiple payment methods into a single configuration.

The Connector is designed to minimize technical effort and to accelerate time-to-market while maintaining high standards of safety and availability.

## How does implementation advance?

<Steps>
  <Step title="Install the connector">
    Download the application to your account VTEX using VTEX IO CLI.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Configure the supplier">
    Registration `App Key`, `App Token` and `Merchant Code` To connect your merchant with Alignet One.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Active your payment methods">
    It enables only means previously contracted and defines necessary commercial conditions.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Test the complete checkout">
    Valids approved and rejected payments before moving integration to production.
  </Step>
</Steps>

***

## Prior requirements

Before we start, make sure you have:

<Check>
  A Store Active at VTEX with administrative access to the payment module.
</Check>

<Check>
  Credentials authorized with Alignet One for `sandbox` or `producción`.
</Check>

<Check>
  Integration keys: **App Key**, **App Token** and **Merchant Code**.
</Check>

<Check>
  Payment methods previously contracted with Alignet One so we can get them to your store.
</Check>

Those credentials shall be provided by the Alignet One.
