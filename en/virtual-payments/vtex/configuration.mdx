## Configure VTEX

***

## 1. Creating a supplier Alignet One

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Route at VTEX Admin" icon="store">
    `Store Settings → Payments → Providers`
  </Card>

  <Card title="What are you gonna do here?" icon="plus">
    You'll create the provider **Alignet One** and then load credentials and enable your merchant's payment methods.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Note>
  If you do not place this option in the side menu, use the top Admin search engine and write `Providers`.
</Note>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enter Providers">
    Go to `Store Settings → Payments → Providers`.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Creating a new supplier">
    Click **New Provider** at the top right.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Search Alignet One">
    In the search window, write **Alignet One** To filter the list.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Select the right provider">
    Choose the supplier **Alignet One** with type **Payment** to continue with the configuration.
  </Step>
</Steps>
